Privacy policy

SITE MAGIQUE respects the privacy of everyone who contacts us and limits data collection to what is necessary to answer enquiries and deliver projects.

Data we collect

We may receive your name, contact details, business information and the material required for your project when you voluntarily contact us.

How data is used

This information is used only to answer enquiries, prepare quotations, deliver and maintain projects, provide support and meet our obligations.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your data. It is shared only with technical providers required to deliver the service and retained for the period needed for our relationship or required by law.

Security

We apply reasonable measures to protect entrusted information and access credentials, although no Internet service can guarantee zero risk.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements.
