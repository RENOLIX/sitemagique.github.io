FAQ

Your questions, answered

Is advertising spend included?

Management fees and the budget paid to Google are separate and agreed before launch.

Can you guarantee sales?

No. Results depend on the market, offer, budget and competition. We set objectives and optimise using observed data.

Can I keep my Google Ads account?

Yes. We can work with the necessary access to your account while you retain visibility into your campaigns.
